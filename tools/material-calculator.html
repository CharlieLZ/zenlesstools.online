FAQ

What is a ZZZ leveling calculator?

A ZZZ leveling calculator estimates the materials, Dennies and farming days needed to level an agent, skill or W-Engine to your target level. Enter a target level and the tool totals the materials and estimated days at 180 stamina per day. Results are planning approximations, not exact in-game costs.

What is the max level in Zenless Zone Zero?

Agents and W-Engines cap at level 60, and each skill caps at level 12. Reaching the higher brackets costs far more Dennies and materials, as shown in the Leveling Costs Reference above.

How accurate is the material calculator?

It provides estimates based on level-to-material ratios. Actual costs vary per agent rarity (S-Rank costs more than A-Rank). Use as a planning guide, not exact numbers.

What should I farm first?

Weekly-gated materials first: Expert Challenge (Core Skill mats) and Notorious Hunt (Ascension Seals). Then Dennies, then Investigation Merits, then W-Engine components.

How long does it take to max an agent?

From level 1 to 60 with all skills at 10, typically 2-3 weeks of dedicated farming (assuming 180 stamina/day + weekly boss mats).
