FAQ

Is this an official ZZZ website?

No. ZZZ Tools Hub is fan-made. Not affiliated with or endorsed by HoYoverse.

How do I redeem ZZZ codes?

In-game: Menu → Settings → Account → Redeem Code. Or via hoyoverse.com/gift. Works on PC, mobile, PS5, and Xbox.

How does pity work in ZZZ?

Soft pity at 75 pulls, hard pity at 90. 50/50 system: if your last 5★ wasn't featured, next is guaranteed. Pity carries between same-type banners.

How often are codes updated?

After version updates, livestreams, and announcements. Check the Last Updated timestamp.

Is this site free?

Yes. All tools are free. No login, no account, no payment required.
