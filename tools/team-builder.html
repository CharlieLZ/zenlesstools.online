Element Pairing

Strong Pairs

  • Ice + Ice — Fast freeze, shatter synergy
  • Ether + Ether — Corrosion stacking
  • Ice + Electric — Freeze + Shock Disorder
  • Fire + Physical — Burn + Assault Disorder

Viable Pairs

  • Ether + Ice — Corrosion + Freeze
  • Electric + Ether — Shock + Corrosion
  • Fire + Ice — Melt-style synergy
  • Physical + Electric — Assault + Shock

Avoid

  • No element synergy in team
  • Too many roles, not enough damage
  • Three supports with no DPS
  • Mismatched element bonuses

FAQ

What's the best team for beginners?

The free starter team: Billy/Corin (DPS), Anby (Stun), and Nicole (Support). All three are given for free and can clear most story content and early Shiyu Defense floors.

Do I need a Stun agent in every team?

Not strictly. Stun is optimal for hypercarry teams, but Anomaly/Disorder teams can work without one. The third slot is flexible based on your roster.

How important is element synergy?

Very important for Anomaly teams (you need different elements for Disorder). Less critical for hypercarry teams where raw damage matters more. Mono-element teams gain bonus Anomaly buildup.
