FAQ

How accurate is the pull planner?

It's an estimate based on the numbers you enter. Daily income varies with events and your playtime. Use conservative estimates and add a 10-20 pull buffer for safety.

What's a good daily pull estimate?

Pure F2P: ~0.5 pulls/day. With monthly pass: ~0.7. Monthly pass + Battle Pass: ~0.8-1.0. Events typically add 10-20 bonus pulls per version.

Should I plan for 75 or 90 pity?

Plan for 80-85 to be safe. Most 5★ drops happen between 75-80 pulls due to soft pity. Planning for 90 is the safest worst-case scenario.
