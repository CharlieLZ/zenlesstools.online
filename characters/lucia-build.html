Lucia Build Guide

Ether · Support · S-Rank · Version 2.8

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Role & Playstyle

Lucia is an Ether Support who provides powerful team-wide buffs, energy regeneration, and healing. Her kit focuses on enabling her teammates rather than dealing personal damage. She fits into almost any team as a flexible support slot and has especially strong synergy with Ether and hybrid-element teams.

Skill Priority

  • Core Passive — Team buffs, max first
  • Special / EX Special — Buff application
  • Ultimate / Chain Attack — AoE buffs + healing
  • Basic Attack — Energy generation
  • Dodge — Last priority

Pull Value

Should you pull Lucia?

Yes if: You lack a premium support, want to build flexible teams, or value account-wide utility characters. Supports have longer shelf life than DPS units.
Maybe if: You already have built supports and prefer pulling for main DPS.
Skip if: You only play one team and are satisfied with current supports.

Overall: High account value. Universal supports like Lucia fit into many teams and stay relevant across versions.

FAQ

Is Lucia F2P friendly?

Very. As a support, Lucia's value comes from her buffs, not her stats. She works well with F2P W-Engines and lower-investment Drive Discs. Her energy-based playstyle means Energy Regen is more important than raw ATK.

Does Lucia need her signature W-Engine?

Not as much as a DPS. Her signature is good but not mandatory. Budget Support W-Engines with Energy Regen work well. Prioritize getting Lucia herself over the W-Engine.

What teams does Lucia work best in?

Lucia is extremely flexible. She fits into any team needing a support slot. She has special synergy with Ether DPS units and teams that rely on Chain Attacks and Ultimates due to 4pc Swing Jazz synergy.
