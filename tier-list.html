FAQ

How are the tiers determined?

Tiers are based on overall performance, team synergy, ease of use, and account value. S-Rank agents are generally the best, A-Rank are strong alternatives, and B-Rank are niche or budget options. This is a community-informed tier list, not official.

Should I only use S-Rank agents?

No. Team synergy matters more than individual rankings. A well-built A-Rank team with good synergy can outperform a messy S-Rank team. Use this tier list as a guide, not a rule.

Do tier lists change between versions?

Yes. New agents, balance changes, and meta shifts can change rankings. This tier list is updated for Version 2.8. Check back after version updates.
