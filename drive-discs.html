Drive Discs Guide

All Drive Disc sets, 2pc/4pc effects, recommended stats, and where to farm them

Last updated: May 7, 2026

How Drive Discs Work

Slots

Each agent has 6 disc slots. Slots 1-3 have fixed main stats (HP, ATK, DEF). Slots 4-6 have variable main stats you can farm for.

Set Bonuses

Equip 2 pieces from the same set for a 2pc bonus. Equip 4 pieces for the 4pc bonus. Most builds use 4pc + 2pc from different sets.

Sub Stats

Each disc has up to 4 random sub stats that upgrade every 3 levels. Farming perfect sub stats is the real endgame.

All Drive Disc Sets

4pc: Landing a CRIT hit on an enemy increases ATK by 9% for 6s. Max 3 stacks.

SupportTeam Buff

2pc: Energy Regen +20%
4pc: After using Chain Attack or Ultimate, all squad members gain 15% increased DMG for 12s.

Hormone Punk

DPSATK

2pc: ATK +10%
4pc: Upon entering combat or switching in, ATK +25% for 10s. 20s cooldown.

Best for: Quick-swap DPS, burst agents

Puffer Electro

PENUtility

2pc: PEN Ratio +8%
4pc: After using Ultimate, PEN Ratio +15% for 8s. ATK +15% if enemies are under Anomaly.

Best for: Rina teams, PEN-based builds

Shockstar Disco

2pc: Impact +6%
4pc: Basic and Dash attacks deal 20% more Daze to the primary target.

Soul Rock

DefenseSurvival

2pc: DEF +15%
4pc: When taking DMG, reduce DMG taken by 40%. 15s cooldown.

Best for: Caesar, Ben, Seth
