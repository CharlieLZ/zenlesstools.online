Promeia Build Guide

Ice · Anomaly · S-Rank · Version 2.8

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Role & Playstyle

Promeia is an Ice Anomaly agent specializing in freeze application and shatter damage. Her kit excels at building Ice Anomaly quickly, triggering frequent freeze reactions for crowd control, and dealing high burst damage when shattering frozen enemies. Best played as the primary on-field DPS in a freeze/shatter composition.

Skill Priority

  • Core Passive — Max first
  • Basic Attack — Main damage source
  • Special / EX Special — Anomaly buildup
  • Chain Attack / Ultimate — Burst damage
  • Dodge — Last priority

Pull Value

Should you pull Promeia?

Yes if: You need an Ice DPS, want to build a freeze/shatter team, or like her playstyle.
Maybe if: You already have a built Ice team (Lycaon, Ellen, Soukaku).
Skip if: You're saving for a future character or don't enjoy Anomaly gameplay.

Overall: Strong pull value for account diversity. Ice Anomaly is a valuable niche.

FAQ

Is Promeia F2P friendly?

Yes. Promeia works well with craftable W-Engines and free characters like Anby and Nicole. Her core damage comes from Anomaly reactions, not raw stats, making her less gear-dependent than ATK-scaling DPS.

Should I pull for her signature W-Engine?

Her signature Frostfall Sickle is best-in-slot and a noticeable upgrade. If you have enough pulls after securing Promeia, it's worth considering. For F2P, craftable and BP W-Engines are functional alternatives.

What Mindscape Cinema level is recommended?

M0 is fully functional. M1 and M2 provide quality-of-life improvements. Stop at M0 unless you're investing heavily.
