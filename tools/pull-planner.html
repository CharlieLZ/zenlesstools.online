FAQ

How accurate is the pull planner?

Current resources are separated from estimated future income. The worst-case budget assumes the standard Exclusive Agent 90-pull and 50/50 rules, based on the pity and guarantee status you enter. Confirm Signal Search → Details in-game; W-Engine, Bangboo, discounted and special channels are not covered.

What's a good daily pull estimate?

Start at zero, then add only rewards you expect to claim. Do not buy a pass or exceed your spending limit to close a calculated gap. Skipping a banner remains an option.

Should I plan for 75 or 90 pity?

90 is the hard-pity budget for one standard S-rank cycle. Lower thresholds are estimates, not guarantees. Without a featured guarantee, the worst-case budget includes a second 90-pull cycle; projected future income is still not owned currency.
